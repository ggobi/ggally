# stat\_\*(): Additional statistics for ggplot2

[GGally](https://ggobi.github.io/ggally/) reexports three
[ggplot2](https://ggplot2.tidyverse.org) statistics functions from
[ggstats](https://larmarange.github.io/ggstats/). Please see their
corresponding vignettes in
[ggstats](https://larmarange.github.io/ggstats/) for more details: \*
[Compute cross-tabulation statistics with
`stat_cross()`](https://larmarange.github.io/ggstats/articles/stat_cross.html)
([`vignette("stat_cross", "ggstats")`](https://larmarange.github.io/ggstats/articles/stat_cross.html))
\* [Compute custom proportions with
`stat_prop()`](https://larmarange.github.io/ggstats/articles/stat_prop.html)
([`vignette("stat_prop", "ggstats")`](https://larmarange.github.io/ggstats/articles/stat_prop.html))
\* [Compute weighted mean with
`stat_weighted_mean()`](https://larmarange.github.io/ggstats/articles/stat_weighted_mean.html)
([`vignette("stat_weighted_mean", "ggstats")`](https://larmarange.github.io/ggstats/articles/stat_weighted_mean.html))
